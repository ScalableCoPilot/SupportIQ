## Initial assumptions and recommendations

Assumptions explicitly labeled in the baseline are captured in [assumptions-register.md](../evidence/assumptions-register.md), not promoted to requirements. No new business assumptions are introduced here.

Recommendations for later discovery (not decisions):

- Confirm the primary user/audience and the first-release priority boundary before detailed feature elaboration.
- Separate development/test deployment expectations from any public or hosted demo requirement.
- Define the access policy and eligible “approved knowledge” before designing acceptance tests for RAG.
- Define measurable AI evaluation and operational thresholds only with owner input; none are invented here.
